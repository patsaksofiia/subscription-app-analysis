# subscription-app-analysis
This project is an analysis of a mobile app for learning English with a subscription model. The company wanted to understand why some users finish the free trial but never pay, and which acquisition channels are actually worth the money.
# Task

The app works like this: a user installs it, signs up, can start a free trial, makes the first payment, renews the subscription, sometimes upgrades to a more expensive plan or asks for a refund. The app launched in 6 countries step by step, starting from February 2024.

Management has two problems:
Some users finish the trial but never pay.
It's unclear which acquisition channels actually pay off and which just waste the budget.

My job as an analyst was to analyze the subscription funnel and unit economics and find out:

where users are lost
which cohorts and channels are the most valuable
how much it costs to get a user (CAC) vs how much revenue they bring (LTV)
what to do to grow revenue, retention and marketing efficiency
